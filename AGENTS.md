The mantra for this project repository is extreme minimalism. Creating is always easier than destroying when it comes to AI agents. That is paradoxical, because surely writing and editing and creating ought to be harder than running deletion commands. In practice, it's the opposite: the simplicity of deletion makes it dangerous, while the complexity of creation makes it safe. 

Likewise, preserving prior work is also safer, than rightfully acknowledging flawed work, and then deleting it or discarding large portions of it. 

The end result is that bad architecture and assumptions get built upon and never destroyed. Hence the necessity for extreme minimalism, and not being afraid to call out bad work.
